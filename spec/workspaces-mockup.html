how this works

Drag the empty desktop to move around this workspace. Windows can sit anywhere, far off to the side too.
Workspaces are the names at the top; + makes a new one, double-click a name to rename it. Swipe sideways with two fingers (three-finger swipes belong to macOS here), press ⌃ ← →, or click a name to switch.
Windows: drag the title bar, drag the corner to resize, double-click the title to fill the screen. Red closes it (nothing is lost, it stays in the sidebar), yellow parks it off to the right, green fills the screen.
Teams: drag a teammate out and it becomes its own window; drop a terminal on a team and it joins. ✎ opens the note of an agent or a team.
A page is a note: type a link at the top of a page and it opens inside the note; write about it underneath.
Sub-workspaces are areas of the desktop. Drag one by its name, ⤢ opens it in its own window (another monitor); close that window and it is still here.
Drop any window on a workspace name to move it there. The dock makes a new agent, team, note, page or sub-workspace. ⌘K searches everything, old sessions included. ⌘\ hides the sidebar.
